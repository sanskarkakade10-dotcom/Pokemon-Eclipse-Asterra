# Story — Pokémon Eclipse

## Premise

Asterra has entered an abnormal period in the Eclipse Cycle. Strange phenomena begin appearing across the region, and Pokémon near affected areas show unusual behavior.

The player starts in Aster Haven and initially believes the adventure will be a normal Pokémon journey. That changes when an early Eclipse event disrupts the town's routine.

## Act I — The First Shadow

- Player begins in Aster Haven.
- Professor Lyra introduces Pokémon research and the region's unusual eclipse history.
- Nova becomes the player's rival.
- The player receives a starter.
- The first journey along Route 1 reveals that Pokémon distribution in Asterra is unusually diverse.
- An unexplained Eclipse-related incident provides the first major mystery.

## Act II — Team Umbra

Team Umbra begins appearing in different parts of Asterra. At first their actions look unrelated, but they are collecting information and artifacts connected to ancient Eclipse sites.

Their ideology: the Eclipse is not a disaster to prevent—it is an opportunity to reshape the relationship between humans and Pokémon.

## Act III — The Black Eclipse

The player discovers that the Black Eclipse is part of a recurring regional phenomenon. Team Umbra attempts to trigger or control it.

Nova's relationship with the player becomes more important as both characters confront different ideas about power, responsibility and what makes a Pokémon partner.

## Endgame

The player must stop Team Umbra, resolve the central Eclipse event and face the Pokémon League.

The final legendary encounter and Champion battle should pay off the region's central themes rather than simply reproduce FireRed's climax.

## Post-game

Planned post-game content includes:

- Eclipse-affected areas reopening
- rare Pokémon encounters
- additional legendary investigations
- Team Umbra remnants
- optional battle challenges
- new routes or islands
- lore discoveries about previous Eclipse cycles
