# Asterra Region

## Region concept

Asterra is a diverse region shaped by unusual geological and atmospheric activity associated with the Eclipse Cycle.

The region begins in the quiet settlement of **Aster Haven** and gradually expands into forests, coastal settlements, mountain passes, ancient ruins and areas affected by the Black Eclipse.

## Starting area

### Aster Haven

The player's home and the starting point of the adventure.

Key concepts:

- Player's home
- Professor Lyra's research facility
- Nova's home
- First hints of the Eclipse Cycle
- Route 1 departure point

### Route 1

The first route introduces Asterra's wider Pokémon ecosystem and serves as the first playable showcase for the multi-generation design.

The route should eventually contain:

- early wild Pokémon
- beginner trainers
- an optional side path
- the first Team Umbra hint
- an environmental clue connected to the Eclipse Cycle

### Eclipse Haven

The first significant settlement after Aster Haven. It will eventually become the first major story hub and the location of the first Gym challenge.

## Future regional structure

The final region will be planned around connected ecological zones rather than simply reskinning Kanto routes.

Planned broad zones:

1. Aster Haven and surrounding countryside
2. Verdant forest region
3. Coastal/eastern settlements
4. Central highlands
5. Ancient Eclipse ruins
6. Northern mountain region
7. League approach
8. Eclipse endgame zone

Exact town names, maps and route connections will be finalized as the map-development phase begins.
