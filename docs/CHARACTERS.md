# Characters

## Protagonist

The player creates/names the protagonist. Their background is intentionally lightweight so the player remains the center of the story.

## Nova — Rival

Nova is the protagonist's rival and childhood friend from Aster Haven.

Nova is competitive, curious and more cautious about the Eclipse phenomenon than the protagonist. Their starter selection will be determined by the player's generation/starter choice.

## Professor Lyra

Lyra is Asterra's Pokémon researcher. Her work focuses on the relationship between Pokémon behavior, regional ecology and the Eclipse Cycle.

She becomes one of the player's main sources of information about the mystery.

## Team Umbra

Team Umbra is the primary villain organization.

Their public-facing activity initially appears scientific and exploratory. Their deeper goal is to control the Black Eclipse and use its effects to permanently alter the balance of power between humans and Pokémon.

The organization should have distinct ranks, recurring characters and an internal ideology rather than functioning as a simple Team Rocket replacement.
